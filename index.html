Quiz Trivia - Privacy Policy & Account Deletion

Last updated: November 2026

Account & Data Deletion

Users have full control over their account data. If you wish to delete your account and associated game data (match history, points, profile):

  • Send an email to ayaantech.contact@gmail.com with the subject "Delete My Account" from your registered Google email address.
  • Your account data, authentication profile, and leaderboard records will be permanently deleted from Firebase within 7 business days.

Information Collection & Usage

Quiz Trivia connects with Google Sign-In via Firebase to create your player profile. We store your display name, player avatar, and match statistics to provide online quiz battles and tournament rankings.

Security & Third-Party Services

We use Google Play Services, Firebase Authentication, Firestore, and Google AdMob to deliver services and ads compliant with Google Play Developer policies.

Contact Us

For any privacy inquiries or support, contact us at: ayaantech.contact@gmail.com
